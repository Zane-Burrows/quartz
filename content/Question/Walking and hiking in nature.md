A Conversation with D about hiking in nature: 

"I realized that I really appreciate hiking because it has that bonus of keeping you in shape without you really having to prioritize that as your sort of raison d'etre for going. It can be like I'm here to get to the top of the mountain, or I'm here to see the pretty trees"

---

It is interesting that some people have a natural desire for movement, yet for some of us it is Type II fun, based on the [[Type I vs type II model of fun]].
- To encourage exercise it is important to reframe the purpose of the exercise. For example, at my partner's pilates studio there is a sign that says, "You are only one workout away from a good mood"
