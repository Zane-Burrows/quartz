When I think about what I am grateful for, it is usually people that come to mind. I am grateful for my partner, my best friend, my sister, and my childhood friend. Yet, I believe gratitude can expend deeper than that. Why am I grateful for these people?

[[My Partner]]

I am grateful for my partner who reminds me to live in the moment.
I am grateful that we can go on trips and make fun memories.
I am grateful for the car that transports us to new places.

[[My Best Friend]]

I am grateful for my best friend who has been a lifelong learner beside me.
I am grateful for our walks and deep talks.
I am grateful for my body that allows us to hike together.

[[My Sister]]

I am grateful for my sister who keeps me laughing. 
I am grateful that we have old photos of childhood memories.
I am grateful for the technology that allowed us to capture these moments.

[[My Childhood Friend]]

I am grateful for my childhood friend who has been a sense of security.
I am grateful that we grew up together in the same city.
I am grateful for my parents who hosted them several times.

Through this I learn there is a lot to be grateful for.
