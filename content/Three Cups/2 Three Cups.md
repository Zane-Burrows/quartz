I think when you love someone you find meaning in doing little things with them, because ultimately it is their company that brings you joy. I will sit on the couch with my partner sipping coffee and feel extremely grateful to be a part of her mundane routine life. It feels almost intimate to see someone outside of their persona for the world doing their daily routine.
