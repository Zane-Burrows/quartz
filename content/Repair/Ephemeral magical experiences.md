> Ephemeral - devoted to what is of temporary interest

> Magical - extremely or extraordinarily pleasant, enjoyable, or exciting

Examples of Ephemeral Magical Experience
- One key ephemeral magical experience from my older adolescence is when I became a camp counselor.
	- Magic - The camp was magical due to community. The counselors and staff uplifted each other to help care for the campers
	- Ephemeral - Because camp was only a week, it made the experience more unique. It heightened the magic of it, before I could become habituated.
- A prime example of ephemeral magical experience in fiction is [[The Night Circus]] by Erin Morgenstern
	- Magic - The circus is filled with exhibitions that have a magical nature, yet are close enough to reality the common viewer may see it as real.
	- Ephemeral - The circus only appears at night traveling across the world.
