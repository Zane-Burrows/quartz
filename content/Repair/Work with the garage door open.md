[[Conversational learning]]
- One benefit of learning in public is the people you connect with along the way.
- Anecdote: I knew someone into crafting. Any tactile medium they either knew or could learn. They heavily advised crafting in public, because of the stories people would share with them. It was a [[Conversational in]] to find like-minded individuals.

[[Steal Like an Artist]]
- "The Secret: Do Good Work and Share It with People"
	- This allows other creators to discover your work and riff off your ideas to create something new.
