The benefits
- One benefit of learning in public is the people you connect with along the way.

> I knew someone into crafting. Any tactile medium they either knew or could learn. They heavily advised crafting in public, because of the stories people would share with them. It was a [[Conversational in]] to find like-minded individuals.
