> A [microadventure ](https://alastairhumphreys.com/blog/microadventures-3/) is an adventure that is short simple, local, cheap - yet still fun, exciting, challenging, refreshing, and rewarding

Coined by Alastair Humphreys. Introduced to me in this video, [How Micro-Adventures May Save Your Creativity & Drive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O7QRlDrjZvc)

---

Conversely to how [[Routine as intentionality]] may reduce mental effort, microadventures break routine placing us in a growth zone.
